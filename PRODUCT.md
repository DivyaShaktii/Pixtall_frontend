# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
E-commerce sellers and marketers creating product listings. They use this tool to generate assets efficiently.

## Product Purpose
To configure generation parameters and review results. Success means generating marketplace-ready creatives quickly and seamlessly.

## Positioning
One-click marketplace-ready creatives without manual prompting.

## Brand Personality
Minimalist, premium, elegant, dark luxury atelier.
References: Midjourney and other creative tools (dark mode, image-forward). The canvas is a deep Onyx to hero generated images, utilizing a single Champagne Gold splash for primary actions. Restraint and depth convey a high-quality professional instrument rather than a flashy consumer app.

## Anti-references
- Not like a generic Bootstrap/Material admin dashboard
- Not overly technical or cluttered with too many sliders

## Design Principles
- **Focus on the Output:** The generated images should be the hero of the interface.
- **Effortless Control:** Provide advanced configuration without overwhelming technical clutter.
- **Premium Feel:** Use elegant minimalism and dark mode to convey a high-quality, professional tool.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
High contrast and keyboard navigation are critical.
